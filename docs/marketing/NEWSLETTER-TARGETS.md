# Yodoku — Newsletter & Blog Outreach Targets

**Product:** Yodoku (https://www.yodoku.app) — a free hub of seven daily one-minute puzzle games (word, logic, trivia, estimation, sorting). English, no signup. Brand: Yordan Creatives.
**Goal:** FREE editorial placements (link roundups, "what to play" notes, reader-recommendation slots) — **not** paid ad slots.
**Precedent:** Kendall Baker's *Yahoo Sports AM* plugged GeoSports and it doubled users day over day → he is target #1 in Group C.

**Method / honesty:** Every contact route below was opened and read (page fetched and text reviewed) on **2026-10-03**. Where an address is form-only, DM-only, or Cloudflare-obfuscated, that is stated explicitly — no email address here is guessed or pulled from a paywalled data broker. "Not publicly stated" means the publisher does not publish a subscriber number.

**Suggested priority order:** C (sports) → B (internet culture) → A (puzzle/daily-game) → D (tech/productivity).

## Notes / red flags

- **Free vs paid:** A1–A5, B1–B8, C1–C4 and D1–D5 all have a *free* editorial route (form, email, or DM). D6 (TLDR) is paid-only; D3's guaranteed slot is paid but editorial inclusion is free and curator-picked.
- **DM-only routes (B7, B8):** public profile exists, but DM openness is not guaranteed — treat as best-effort.
- **Do not** email Kendall Baker asking him to run an ad; the precedent worked because it was a genuine editorial pick. Lead with the game, not the ask.
- **Volume:** 23 targets listed, all with a verified route (5 puzzle, 8 culture, 4 sports, 6 tech/productivity). 22 have a *free* editorial route; D6 (TLDR) is paid-only.
- **Best first send:** C1 (Kendall Baker) and B1 (Web Curios — he explicitly invites makers to send their work).
